## Limitations
- Session network policy blocked DefiLlama, CoinGecko, L2Beat, EIP pages, Coinbase/Privy/Turnkey docs, x402.org. All market figures are from search-result summaries and secondary sites and were not verified against primary data.
- Web search results can be wrong or SEO-driven; deal values for Lakera, Protect AI and Privy are estimates.
- No competitor product was tested.
- The repository was not read line by line (see `repository-audit.md` section 0).
