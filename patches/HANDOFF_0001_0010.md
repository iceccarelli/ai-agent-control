### Patch 0010 — the multiple-testing layer (their finding, corrected and built)

`tools/deflated_sharpe.py` + `tools/hypothesis_registry.py`:

- `family_wise_false_positive_rate` — reproduces the tree's own 14% at width 3
- `expected_max_sharpe_under_null` — Bailey & López de Prado 2014 eq. 5,
  **validated against Monte Carlo at widths 3/12/50, within 1%**
- `deflated_sharpe_ratio` — corrects for trials, skew, kurtosis, sample length
- `minimum_track_record_length` — "could n=41 *ever* support this claim?"
- `effective_n_trials` — correlation-adjusted, **not** the default: twelve
  families over one price path are not independent, so the raw count
  over-corrects, which is the safe direction
- append-only registry, seeded retroactively, refuses overwrites

**A bug I found in my own implementation, left visible in the commit message
because it is the instructive part**: `sr` is per-observation while the
order statistic is z-scale. Subtracting them directly — the naive reading of
the paper — compares ~0.3 against a bar of ~1.66 and returns `dsr = 0` for
*every* input. An instrument that looks like rigour and discriminates
nothing. Fixed (scale by `1/sqrt(n-1)`), with a regression test. Post-fix:
noise 0.15, real signal 0.70, same signal with one fat tail 0.29.

## 3. The one thing I want the next session to take seriously

Their Stage-2 analysis is correct and it reframes everything. Combined with
the economics already reproduced in round 3:

```
search width                    12 families, one price path, one window
P(≥1 clears | global null)      46.0%
setups                          485 (32.9% of days)
  SHORT                         477 (98.4%)
  at exactly the threshold      374 (77.1%)   ← Binance's default funding clamp
carry over horizon              15 bps vs 25 bps round trip → does not pay
buy-and-hold, same window       +229.78%
forward                         n=2, mean net R −0.8358
```

Read together: a near-permanent short on an asset that tripled, triggered
77% of the time by a venue's *default* funding value, funded by a carry that
does not cover costs, selected from twelve trials at a width where a 46%
false-positive rate is expected. That is not "an unproven edge awaiting
forward data." **The most parsimonious reading is that there was never an
edge, and the forward losses are the null asserting itself.**

The honest next step is not to wait seven months for n=20. It is to run the
registry and DSR against the Stage-1 return stream and let the number say
so. That is one session's work and it is the highest-value thing available.

**Not done, deliberately**: Combinatorial Purged CV. `HORIZON=5` and
`LOCKUP=1` are the right purge/embargo parameters, but replacing the
pre-declared OOS cut is itself a new trial on an already-seen window, and
it must not ride along in the same patch as the counter that would have to
count it.

## 5. On the capacity question

Their estimate is the right shape and I agree with it: BTCUSDT USDT-M median
volume ~$11.5bn/day from this corpus; a world-class systematic crypto book on
one venue realistically tops out around **$100m–$2bn AUM**; Medallion is
capped near $10bn and returns capital annually because capacity binds before
skill does. That is the honest ceiling, it is a real achievement if reached,
and it is four orders of magnitude below "trillion". Aim the harness there.

The asset here is the refusal harness. It is currently welded to a signal
that the numbers say never worked. Pointing it at a genuinely new information
set — with the trial counted *before* the result is seen — is the only
honest next step, and all three audits now independently agree on that.
